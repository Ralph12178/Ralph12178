### 🎵 One Ministry. One Purpose. In Sync.

</div>

**Synced N** is an Android mobile application designed to help church music ministries manage their activities in one centralized platform.

It helps ministry leaders, musicians, and singers stay organized, connected, and in sync through features designed for worship team coordination.

#### ✨ Key Features

* 📅 **Worship Scheduling** — Organize worship sessions and ministry schedules.
* 👥 **Team Roles** — Assign responsibilities to team members.
* 🎶 **Song Lineups** — Arrange songs for worship sessions.
* 📝 **Lyrics & Chords** — Share song lyrics and chord information.
* 💬 **Team Communication** — Keep ministry members connected.
* 🔔 **Real-Time Updates** — Receive updates about schedules and changes.

#### 🎯 Project Goal

To make ministry coordination easier by bringing worship schedules, team responsibilities, song information, and communication together in one convenient application.

> *One Ministry. One Purpose. In Sync.*

**My interest:** Creating thoughtful, user-friendly interfaces that support the needs of ministry leaders and team members.
